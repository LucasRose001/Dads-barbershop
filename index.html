About

One chair. One barber. One dad.

Dad's Barbershop opened in 2024 in Martensville. It's a one-man shop — no assembly line, no upsells, no rush. Just an appointment on the books and a barber who's happy you're in the chair.

Between school pickups and hockey practice, cutting hair is what I love doing. Come in, sit down, and let's get you sorted.
