About

One chair. One barber. One dad.

Dad's Barbershop opened in Martensville in 2024. It's Austin Giovanni's shop — a professional barber, a local dad, and the whole team, all in one. That's where the name comes from.

No assembly line. No upsells. No rush. Just an appointment on the books, a proper cut, and usually a pretty good conversation while you're in the chair. Bring the kids too — Austin's especially good with first cuts and toddlers who aren't sure about the whole thing yet.

  • Locally owned and operated in Martensville, SK
  • Great with kids, teens, adults, and first-timers
  • Reuzel · STMNT · Suavecito products available in-shop
